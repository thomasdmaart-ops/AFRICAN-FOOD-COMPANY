About African Foods Company Ltd

Powering South Africa's agricultural supply chain through cold-chain logistics, quality food production, and enterprise distribution.

Sustaining Nations, Connecting Markets

Established as a premier South African agricultural and food distribution enterprise, African Foods Company Ltd bridges the gap between farm producers, wholesale distributors, and commercial retail markets[cite: 7].

Through our interconnected multi-region distribution network, we manage the seamless procurement, cold-chain storage, processing, and delivery of high-quality poultry, dairy products, grains, and fresh farm produce across Southern Africa.

🎯 Our Mission

To deliver fresh, nutritious, and affordable food products across South Africa by leveraging advanced cold-chain technology, secure enterprise infrastructure, and sustainable agricultural partnerships.

👁️ Our Vision

To be Southern Africa's most reliable and technologically integrated food supply chain network, driving food security and operational excellence across all nine provinces.

🛡️ Quality Assurance

Operated strictly under internationally recognized ISO 22000 and HACCP food safety management standards, guaranteeing temperature integrity and quality from farm to shelf.
