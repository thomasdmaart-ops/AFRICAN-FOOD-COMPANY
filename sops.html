Standard Operating Procedures & Infrastructure Governance

Official IT operational procedures, security policies, and cold-chain network management standards for Project NetBridge enterprise sites.

SOP-01

Site-to-Site IPsec VPN & Regional Tunnel Governance

Scope: Cape Town HQ, Durban, Gqeberha, and Polokwane branch locations.

  • Encrypted Tunnels: All inter-site traffic between branch FortiGate firewalls and Cape Town HQ must route through AES-256 encrypted IPsec VPN tunnels.
  • Centralized DHCP: Branch DHCP scopes are managed centrally from the Cape Town core network stack. Local overrides are strictly prohibited without IT Ops sign-off.
  • Redundancy: Dual-WAN failover links must be tested on the 1st Saturday of each month at 02:00 SAST.
SOP-02

Sage ERP & Central Database Access Control

Scope: Database Administrators, Finance, and Stock Management Personnel.

  • Authentication: Access to primary Sage ERP and SQL database servers hosted at Cape Town HQ requires Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
  • Session Timeouts: Inactive user sessions on requisition and inventory terminals automatically terminate after 15 minutes of inactivity.
SOP-03

Cold-Chain IoT Sensor & Temperature Logging Protocol

Scope: Regional Cold-Storage Facilities (Gqeberha, Durban, Cape Town, Polokwane).

  • Telemetry Intervals: Refrigerator and freezer telemetry sensors must log ambient temperatures every 30 seconds to the local gateway.
  • Threshold Escalation: Temperature deviations above -18°C in frozen bays or above 4°C in fresh dairy bays trigger immediate SMS/Email alerts to facility supervisors and regional IT support.
SOP-04

Disaster Recovery & Daily Database Backup Routine

Scope: Central Datacenter Operations & Systems Administration.

  • Backup Schedule: Full database backups execute daily at 23:00 SAST; transaction log backups run hourly between 06:00 and 18:00 SAST.
  • Offsite Replication: Backups are encrypted and replicated to secondary cloud storage and verified for integrity weekly.
