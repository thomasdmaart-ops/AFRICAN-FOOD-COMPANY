SOP 4: IT Security Breach & Cyber Incident Management

Target Scope: Enterprise Cybersecurity, All Endpoints & Network Environments

1. Specific Purpose & Objective

Defines containment, forensic logging, user notification, and system restoration procedures during ransomware attacks, data leaks, or unauthorized access attempts.

2. Operational Importance

Protects proprietary food processing recipes, supplier pricing contracts, and employee personal data under South Africa's POPIA Act. Standardized containment limits ransomware spread and preserves evidentiary audit trails.

3. Mandatory Response Workflow

  1. 15-Minute Mandatory Escalation: Any user or technician detecting suspicious file encryption, unexpected login prompts, or phishing attempts must report it within 15 minutes to security@africanfoods.co.za.
  2. Immediate System Isolation: IT Security Analysts are authorized to isolate infected workstations or entire branch subnets from the network immediately upon detecting active ransomware activity.
  3. Forensic Logging & Containment: Preserve memory dumps and audit logs for forensic analysis prior to endpoint re-imaging.
  4. System Restoration: Re-build compromised endpoints using golden corporate images after full malware eradication confirmation.

4. Legal & Regulatory Obligations (POPIA)

  • Regulatory Disclosure: Any security breach involving customer, employee, or supplier PII must be formally reported to the South African Information Regulator and affected parties in full compliance with POPIA guidelines.
  • Executive Briefing: The Cybersecurity & Compliance Lead must brief the CIO and Legal Officer within 2 hours of confirming an enterprise data leak.
