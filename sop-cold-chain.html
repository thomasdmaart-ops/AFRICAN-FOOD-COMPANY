SOP 1: Cold-Chain & Inventory Tracking System Management

Target Scope: Core Supply Chain, Regional Cold-Storage Warehouses & Distribution Hubs

1. Specific Purpose & Objective

Establishes mandatory technical protocols for maintaining, syncing, calibrating, and auditing IoT temperature sensors, handheld barcode scanners, and central inventory databases across all regional distribution hubs.

2. Operational Importance

Prevents food spoilage, bulk stock loss, and regulatory non-compliance by maintaining live telemetry and real-time inventory tracking. Unhandled system failures without standardized recovery protocols lead to stock discrepancies, delivery delays, and major financial losses.

3. Step-by-Step Technical Procedure

  1. Daily IoT Sensor Audit: Level 2 Systems Technicians must perform daily diagnostic checks on cold-storage warehouse IoT telemetry devices at 06:00 AM.
  2. Barcode Scanner Provisioning: All ruggedized handheld barcode terminals must sync with local warehouse Wi-Fi endpoints and verify active sessions with the central ERP database before shift startup.
  3. Data Telemetry Syncing: Temperature and humidity telemetry data must automatically upload to the central database at 5-minute intervals. If connection drops, local storage caching holds records up to 72 hours.
  4. Fault Escalation: Sensor drift (>±0.5°C threshold) or offline telemetry triggers an immediate P2 ticket to the Service Desk for technician site dispatch.

4. Governance & Compliance Standards

  • HACCP Traceability: All batch movements and temperature logs must maintain unbroken electronic audit trails for regulatory compliance.
  • POPIA Data Integrity: Vendor and driver shipping details processed on warehouse terminals must adhere to encryption standards (AES-256).
